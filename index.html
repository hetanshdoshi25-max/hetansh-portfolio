02 / The Journey
02 — The Journey

Never been someone who can sit idle

I'm Hetansh Doshi, 19, from Mumbai. Currently doing Computer Engineering, but honestly that's just one piece of what I do. I've always wanted to build, experiment, lead, and turn ideas into reality, in whatever form that takes.

2020 · Grade 8

A Discord server, started for fun

It grew to 8,000+ members. I was running esports tournaments and events on it, figuring out leadership and community building way before I knew that's what it was called. That phase changed how I looked at technology and the internet entirely.

Post 12th

Into the creative side

Social media marketing, content creation, direction, design, photoshoots. I don't like sticking to one thing, so I kept doing multiple things at once instead of picking a lane. Scored 96 percentile in JEE along the way, but marks were never really the point for me.

Somewhere between

Teaching myself to code

Mostly YouTube, online resources, and figuring stuff out myself. Curiosity about intelligent assistants slowly turned into actually writing the thing. Python and JavaScript became the tools I use to solve problems nobody asked me to solve.

Now

Building Aethon into a company

An AI assistant I'm building solo, now working toward turning it into a real product people use. Alongside it, I still run client accounts and creative work. Fitness and discipline stay non-negotiable, because how you train your body shapes how you show up everywhere else.

I'm not trying to just watch the future happen. I want to be the one building it.

03 / Aethon
03 — The Startup

Aethon

Founder & Developer. An AI assistant built to talk, see, and act, not just chat back.

Aethon started with one obsession: Iron Man's JARVIS. Not just the idea of it, but actually building something close to it, alone.

I'm the sole founder and developer. No team, no co-founder. Every line of code, every design decision, every deployment call has been on me. Built primarily in Python and JavaScript, Aethon and its desktop assistant Zyra form a system designed to hold a conversation, watch your screen, and actually do things on your machine.

What started as curiosity turned into self-taught coding through YouTube and constant experimentation, and eventually into a working product I'm now building toward a real company.

The goal has never been just another AI chatbot. It's an assistant that can see your screen, hear you, and get things done, all while ensuring complete privacy and safety for the user.

What it does

Nine modes, one assistant
🎙 Voice + Vision Default
  • Natural voice conversation, Zyra replies in her own voice
  • Camera vision, ambient and proactive, sees before being asked
  • PC control: open apps, volume, brightness, media, screen lock, screenshots
  • Screen Vision: say the word, it reads your screen and explains it
  • Watch Me Work: passive screen tips, off by default
🎙 Meeting Mode

Record, transcribe, then hand back a summary with action items.

📋 Task Manager

Add and complete tasks, by voice or by hand.

✍️ Writer

Drafts emails, messages and posts in your voice.

🤖 Ask

AI chat with images, file support, and memory that carries over.

📚 Ask My Docs

Upload a PDF or text file and question it directly.

🎧 Co-pilot

Live meeting support with quick-ask and auto-listen.

🎯 Focus Coach

Pomodoro sessions with a camera-based mood check.

💼 Sales Co-pilot

Live objection radar during calls, plus an automatic wrap-up.

All vision and screen data is processed with user consent and privacy-first design principles.
